Software

From need to product. Down to the code.

Technical skills are part of how I approach business. They let me connect people’s needs to product decisions, assess feasibility and take part in building the solution.

I write code and work on the decisions that come before and after it: what is worth building, for whom, with which priorities and how to maintain it. My interest in physics is there too, in wanting to understand mechanisms and their limits.

Decisions before code

  • Clarify the need, the people we’re building for and the intended outcome.
  • Choose the scope of a first version and what to postpone.
  • Assess feasibility, costs and maintenance alongside the user experience.
  • Consider readability and accessibility in product decisions.

Products and settings I contribute to

  • Into ISICOApp, supporting clinical and rehabilitation journeys.
  • Into Assistudio’s tools for clients, claims and agency work.
  • Into the websites and applications supporting the Palio’s organisation.
  • Into family projects, from homes to the children’s calendar and school.

One example: ISICOApp

I work on an app used by patients and professionals. That means thinking about clear steps, text size and the experience on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. These are aspects to keep working on, listening to the needs of the people using the app.
