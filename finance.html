Finance

Understanding choices about the future.

I’m interested in how numbers connect to decisions: time, risk, available resources and the effects of our choices. That curiosity also shapes the way I approach business.

A project requires choosing where to put time and resources, which assumptions to make and how much room to leave for uncertainty. I want to make those lines of reasoning explicit and comparable, including through the tools I build.

Questions that interest me

  • What resources does a decision require, now and over time?
  • What assumptions sit behind a number or a simulation?
  • How much room are we allowing for uncertainty?
  • How do we make the reasoning clear to the people deciding?

Numbers and tools

  • Gather information that helps describe a situation.
  • Make the assumptions behind a simulation explicit.
  • Use software to make complex questions easier to explore.
  • Read results with the model’s limits in mind.

I’m interested in numbers.
And even more in the questions
behind them.

How it connects to the rest

  • To insurance, through the relationship between choices, risk and protection.
  • To software, through turning a calculation into a usable tool.
  • To business, through decisions made with limited resources and information.
  • To everyday life, because different needs sit behind each scenario.
