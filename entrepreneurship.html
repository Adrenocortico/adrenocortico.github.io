Entrepreneurship

Business means connecting decisions.

I work on direction, people and product. I try to turn a need into a sustainable project, following the decisions and the work needed to carry it forward.

What interests me is the connection between an idea and the reality of an organisation: what we choose to do, with whom, with which resources and with which responsibilities. Being able to develop software helps me assess how to build it too.

Today’s work

I’m a partner in Assistudio Vigevano and a partner and insurance agent at Scegli Noi. At Scegli Noi, the business network has grown into a company with 10 insurance agents: a journey connecting agency work, professional collaboration and organisational decisions.

Insurance Projects

Scegli Noi / partner and insurance agent

I’m a partner and insurance agent at Scegli Noi. It began in 2021 as a network of agencies in Lombardy and Veneto and has become a company with 10 insurance agents.

I’m interested in the move from collaboration between separate businesses to a shared organisation: exchanging experience, making responsibilities clear and providing continuity for clients.

Insurance work is at its core, alongside collaboration with other professionals, including financial advisory and long-term rental services.

The decisions I work on

  • Identify the need and decide whom we want to help.
  • Choose priorities with time, resources and financial sustainability in mind.
  • Connect people’s needs to product and organisational decisions.
  • Follow execution and revisit assumptions in light of the results.

The people I build with

  • Share the reasons behind decisions and the goals we’re working towards.
  • Clarify responsibilities, room for autonomy and limits, including my own.
  • Listen to people who know a problem from a different angle.
  • Create the conditions to work together and recognise others’ contributions.
