Scegli Noi / partner and insurance agent

I’m a partner and insurance agent at Scegli Noi. It began in 2021 as a network of agencies in Lombardy and Veneto and has become a company with 10 insurance agents.

I’m interested in the move from collaboration between separate businesses to a shared organisation: exchanging experience, making responsibilities clear and providing continuity for clients.

Insurance work is at its core, alongside collaboration with other professionals, including financial advisory and long-term rental services.
