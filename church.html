Faith

Faith grows through encounters too.

I’m a catechist in my parish. Accompanying young people is an important part of my life: time to listen, ask questions and explore together.

Faith shapes how I relate to others and the questions I ask myself. I don’t have a ready answer for everything. I want to be present, including when a conversation is difficult or remains open.

My involvement

  • Serving as a catechist with the young people in my parish.
  • Meeting and discussing the questions they bring.
  • Taking part in the life of the community.
  • Mass and the time I make for faith in my week.

The young people’s questions

  • What does the Gospel have to do with our lives?
  • How do we face doubts, choices and relationships?
  • How do we make room for someone who finds it harder to speak?
  • What can I learn from the way they see things?

Some questions
are worth walking
with others.

What I try to bring

  • Listening, including when I don’t immediately share a point of view.
  • Words that are understandable and connected to the life we’re living.
  • A willingness to have my own ideas questioned.
  • My practical skills, when they can help the community.
