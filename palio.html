Palio

A celebration we build together.

The Palio of Vigevano is one way I take part in my city’s life: through its people, its history and the work that brings everyone together each year.

I’m president of Associazione Sforzinda, which organises the Palio of Vigevano. Alongside the organisational work, I build digital tools. It means moving between meetings and practical issues on the ground, with people giving their time.

The people behind it

  • The contrade, with their people and their stories.
  • The volunteers and contributors looking after the many aspects of the event.
  • Institutions, sponsors and local organisations.
  • The participants and visitors who make the Palio a celebration for the city.

My contribution
